One shop receipt that sums up Friday 20 November 2026 in 35 stories from 7 regions — the day's biggest news, written and drawn for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120). Every story on the receipt happened, or was first reported, on that Friday; the Thursday stories that look like Friday's are listed in the receipt's "NOT ON THIS RECEIPT" box as left out.

## The receipt

- A header with the date and the three biggest stories of the day.
- Seven sections, one per region, each line a story with its source and a key figure on the right: Europe 7, Asia 9, Middle East 3, Africa 4, Americas 5, Oceania 3, Global 4.
- A total line: 35 stories, 7 regions, Friday 20 November 2026.
- A "NOT ON THIS RECEIPT" box and a footer stating the date rule.
- A barcode and receipt number as decoration.

## Date check — what is NOT on this receipt

These big stories are deliberately left out because they happened on
Thursday 19 November, not Friday 20 November:

- The Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen
- The ICJ verdict that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
- Anthropic's debut on the Nasdaq
- The GTA VI midnight launch
- The Van Gogh painting sale for $190.4 million
- Voyager 1 confirmed one light-day from Earth
- The US Senate vote on Iran war powers

Every story on the receipt was checked against its first report date
before it was written in; anything dated Thursday was dropped.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# writes out/entry.png — a byte-identical copy of entry.png
```

`main.ts` needs only the Node standard library and no network access.
The image itself is `entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG), drawn with Python/PIL;
every headline, summary, and drawing on it is original work for this entry.
